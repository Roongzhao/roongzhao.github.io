I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the Research School of Economics, Australian National University (ANU). My Ph.D. committee includes José A. Rodrigues-Neto (Chair, ANU), Idione Meneghel (ANU), Ruitian Lang (ANU), Ronald Stauber (ANU), and Han Bleichrodt (University of Alicante).

I expect to complete my Ph.D. in 2027. **I am on the 2026–2027 Economics Job Market.**

My research focuses on microeconomic theory and applied game theory, particularly household decision-making, strategic interactions, and welfare economics. I also study status competition, matching, and economic policy. In addition, I conduct research in behavioral and experimental economics, focusing on the measurement of preferences under risk and uncertainty.

My job market paper, *Time and Income Allocation in Non-cooperative and Cooperative Families*, examines how cooperation within households affects the allocation of time and income. It shows that cooperation can amplify welfare conflicts arising from changes in earning capacity.
