I am a Ph.D. candidate in Economics at the Research School of Economics, Australian National University (ANU). My Ph.D. committee includes José A. Rodrigues-Neto (Chair, ANU), Han Bleichrodt (University of Alicante), Idione Meneghel (ANU), Ruitian Lang (ANU), and Ronald Stauber (ANU).

I expect to complete my Ph.D. in 2027. **I am on the 2026–2027 Economics Job Market.**

My research focuses on applied economic topics using applied game theory. I study household decision-making, status goods, and related policy, such as training programs, childcare subsidy, and taxation. In addition, I conduct research in behavioral and experimental economics, focusing on the measurement of preferences under risk and uncertainty.

My job market paper, *Time and Income Allocation in Non-cooperative and Cooperative Families*, examines how cooperation within households affects the allocation of time and income. It shows that cooperation can amplify welfare conflicts arising from changes in earning capacity.
